Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Nabz-Tech Eats ("we", "us") operates an online food ordering and delivery service in the Kingdom of Bahrain. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our app, we collect the following information:

  • Your name — to personalize your orders and identify you to the restaurant
  • Your phone number — to confirm orders, contact you about your delivery, and let you log in
  • Your delivery address — to deliver your order to the correct location
  • Your GPS location (optional) — only when you tap "Share Location", to help the driver find you faster
  • Your order history — items ordered, prices, and dates, for record-keeping and reordering
  • Payment method preference — cash, Benefit Pay, or card (we do NOT store card numbers)
  • Basic device information — browser type and IP address, for security and troubleshooting

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not store credit or debit card numbers
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or files
  • We do not track your location in the background
  • We do not collect personal information from children under 13

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Process and deliver your food orders
  • Contact you about your order status or delivery
  • Improve our service and menu
  • Send occasional updates (only with your consent)

4. We Never Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. Ever.

5. Who We Share Your Information With

We only share your information with:

  • The restaurant preparing your food
  • The delivery driver assigned to your order
  • Payment processors — only if you pay online
  • Legal authorities — only if required by Bahraini law

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Order records: 3 years (for accounting and legal requirements)
  • Customer account: until you request deletion
  • GPS location: never stored — passed to the driver only for the delivery

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • View the data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and personal data
  • Opt out of marketing messages at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Data Security

We protect your data using industry-standard measures including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all traffic
  • Password-protected systems with access controls
  • Limited staff access on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular encrypted backups

9. Cookies

We use only essential cookies to remember your cart and preferences. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party analytics that would identify you.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date. Continued use of our service after changes means you accept the new policy.
